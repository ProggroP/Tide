TITLE

Tide


TAGLINE

A big hour, a sweeping minute arc and the date, in your colours.


DESCRIPTION

Tide keeps the time quiet and graphic: one large hour number in the centre, the minutes as an arc that fills around the edge, and a small date below.

The minute arc has a soft track and a bright marker at the current minute, so the time is readable at a glance without a second hand.

Tide is built for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2. The layout adapts to the rectangular and the round screen, and the hour follows the watch's 12 or 24 hour setting.


SETTINGS

- Background - the face colour.
- Hour and date - the colour of the hour and the date.
- Accent colour - the colour of the minute arc and its marker.

12 or 24 hour format follows the watch setting.
